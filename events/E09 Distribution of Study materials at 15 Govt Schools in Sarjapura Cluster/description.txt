This time we focused on government schools in the Sarjapur cluster. Our team supported schools in Billapura, Adigera Kallahalli, Tyavakanahalli, Medahalli, Narasepura, Arenuru, Handenahalli, Silk Farm, Sultan Palya, and Bikkanahalli — covering 15 government schools with a total of 1,050 students.

We distributed around 1,000 notebooks, 200 crayons, 30 Jamkanas, 30 dictionaries, and 30 atlas sets to the students.
