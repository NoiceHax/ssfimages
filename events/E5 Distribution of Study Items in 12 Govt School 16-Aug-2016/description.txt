Our team supported government schools in and around Sarjapur — including Mugulur, Tindlu, Matnahalli, Kugur, Panditana Agrahara, Maadappanahalli, Kaamanahalli, Rajiv Gandhi Nagar, Mahal Chowdenahalli, Dodda Timmasandra, Bhovipalya, and Kutaganahalli — covering 12 government schools with a total of 511 students.

We distributed around 1,000 notebooks, 200 crayons, 30 Jamkanas, 30 dictionaries, and 30 atlas sets.
