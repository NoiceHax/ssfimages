Nandha Gokula Ashram for Orphan Girls in Muguluru is managed by a single person who selects girls from underprivileged families — around 70 girls from northeastern states of India — and brings them to the ashram. The ashram provides free education till high school with free food and stay.

We donated rice, oil, dal, fruits, towels, Jamkana, and other kitchen items to support the daily needs of the residents.
