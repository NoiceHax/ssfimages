Sipani Seva Sadan is a home for care-deprived, poverty-stricken individuals, and also for the physically and mentally challenged, which currently has around 300-bed capacity. We chose to extend our help by donating dresses for all the inmates, primarily because of their service towards the poor and destitute.

We presented T-shirts and shorts for men, and nightwear for women — covering all 300 inmates.
